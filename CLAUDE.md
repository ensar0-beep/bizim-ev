# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this is

"Bizim Ev" — a Turkish-language family organizer PWA. The whole app (markup, CSS, JS) lives in `index.html`; there is no build step, package manager or test suite. `sw.js` caches the app shell, `manifest.json` + `icons/` make it installable. Deployed with GitHub Pages from `main` (root).

## Running / developing

- Serve the folder statically (`python3 -m http.server`) and open it. A service worker only registers over http(s), not `file://`.
- Data sync goes to the Firebase Realtime Database in `FB_URL` (top of the "Durum" section). With `FB_URL` empty the app only offers the local "örnek aile" demo.
- For local testing without touching the real database, point `FB_URL` at a small REST+SSE mock (GET/PUT/PATCH on `/families/{code}.json`, and `text/event-stream` GET that sends `put`/`patch` events like Firebase).
- When shipping a change that the service worker must pick up, bump `CACHE` in `sw.js`.

## Architecture

**Modes.** `store.mode` is `null` (welcome screen), `'demo'` (seeded sample family from `seed()`, local only) or `'family'` (synced). `store` is persisted to localStorage under `bizim-ev.v1` and also caches the last remote tree (`store.R`) and unsent writes (`store.pending`), so the app opens instantly and works offline.

**State and rendering.** `S` is the in-memory view: `{meta, ann, members[], events[], tasks[], lists[], items[], wishes[], activity[], freq[], msgs[]}`. Views are template-string functions (`vToday`, `vCal`, `vLists`/`vWishes`, `vTasks`, `vFamily`) rendered into `#screen` by `render()`. Clicks are delegated through `data-act` → the `A` actions object; form submits through `data-form` → `FORMS`. Bottom sheets (`sheet()`) hold every form and detail view. Mutations change `S` directly and then call `render()`.

**Day agenda.** Bugün and Takvim list days one under another (`flowHTML`; empty days collapse into one dashed row). Tasks sit among events on their due day, sorted by time (`tasksOn`, `cTaskRow`); overdue open tasks move to today and done tasks stay on the day they were done. A sticky week strip (`strip`) sits above the list and `trackStrip()` (on `#screen` scroll and after each render) marks the day at the top; in Takvim it also moves to the next week. The month view lists the month's days under the grid, and tapping a day scrolls to it (`UI.pending` → `scrollToDay`). An event can last several days: `end` is its last day (≤60 days, checked in `norm()`); `evLen`/`evStart`/`evIdx` and `occurs()` (via `occ1`) handle it, repeats included. Flow rows show "2. gün" and when it ends (`evSpan`); reminders and Sıradaki use only the first day. In the month grid, multi-day events and holidays are bars joined across the days of a week (`spanSlots`, `.mb.sp`). Swiping the month grid sideways changes the month, and swiping the flow's week strip changes the week (`MS`/`endMs` → `calMove` / `stripWeek`; both are `touch-action:pan-y`).

**Sync (family mode).** Firebase layout: `families/{CODE}/{meta, ann, members/{id}, events/{id}, tasks/{id}, lists/{id}, items/{id}, wishes/{id}, activity/{id}, freq/{id}, msgs/{memberId}, wcm/{id}}`. `render()` ends with `commit()`, which flattens `S` to `collection/id` paths (`flat()`), diffs each record against the last known version (`baseC`, canonical JSON via `canon()`), queues changed/removed paths in `store.pending`, and `flush()` sends them in one multi-path `PATCH`. Failed sends retry with backoff and on the `online` event. Incoming changes arrive via `EventSource` on the family path (`connect()`); `put`/`patch` events are applied to `store.R`, then `fromRemote()` rebuilds `S` from `store.R` plus `store.pending`. The stream is re-opened (fresh token) when it closes for good (`esLater`, backoff 5–60 s), after more than 10 s in the background, on `online`/`pageshow`, and when nothing (not even Firebase's `keep-alive`) arrived for 2 minutes. Remote data is untrusted: `norm()` validates every record (ids, dates, enums, colors) before it reaches the templates — keep that in place when adding fields, and always `esc()` user text.

**Shared tasks.** A task can have several people in `who` (`assignee` stays `who[0]`, rotation uses it). By default one of them finishing completes it; with `all` each person ticks their own part (`dn`) and it completes when everyone has. Use `ws(t)` / `isFor(t,id)` instead of reading `assignee` directly.

**Listeler header.** Alınacaklar and İstekler sit on a camera-style strip (`lTop`, `rtPaint`): the selected one is centred, the other shows faded on both sides, and dragging the strip, tapping the other one, or swiping the list past its first/last sub-tab (`tsTarget` → `rtGo`) switches sides with a spring and a page push. `--buy`/`--wish` colours blend through `--mx` while dragging.

**Adding list items.** Both + buttons (Listeler and Bugün › Alınacak) open the same sheet (`lAddSheet`; `quickItem` just calls it): a name box, a ⋯ button and the + button. ⋯ (`addMore`) opens the details below (`addDet`: list by icon, person like the event/task pickers, `PRI`, note) and hides the suggestions; the hint line (`addHint`) sums up what was picked. `addApply()` puts the details on the next item(s) added and resets them; the chosen list stays.

**Bought items.** Under each list, "Alınanlar" (collapsed, `UI.doneOpen`) groups bought items by when they were bought (`donePer`: Bu hafta, Geçen hafta, Bu ay, then month names; no `doneAt` → Daha eski). The newest group is open, the others are closed and show the first names (`dpPrev`); open/closed per group is `UI.dpo`, reset on list change. Rows (`dnRow`) say who bought it (`doneBy`, set by `toggleItem`, string-checked in `norm()`) and the day (`dDay`); the item detail shows the same.

**Trip plans.** `yer` (Gezi) wishes may carry `wish.trip = {g, b, k:{u,k,y,g}, r[], v, m}` (days, budget, budget split ulaşım/konaklama/yemek/gezi from `TK`, route stops, transport from `TV`, season from `TM`); `norm()` clamps the numbers, keeps ≤30 stops of ≤80 chars and drops the field on other types. In the form `tripForm()` stays a single "Gezi planı ekle" row until tapped; the split budget is behind "Kalemlere ayır". The detail (`tripHTML`) shows the total and a bar, and the split legend opens on tap (`tpBud`). Rows show `tripSum()` ("3 gün · 18.000 ₺"); "Rotayı haritada aç" is a plain Google Maps directions URL (`routeUrl`). General rule from the user: avoid crowding, so extra details open on tap.

**Places in wishes.** For `mekan`/`yer` wishes, `wish.place = {n, a, u}` (name, address, Google Maps link). It arrives through the manifest `share_target` (Google Maps → Paylaş → Bizim Ev opens `./?text=…`, parsed by `parsePlace()` at boot) or a pasted link in the wish form. No Maps API key: detail buttons use plain Google Maps URLs (`mapsUrl`, `dirUrl`). `okMapsUrl()` only accepts https Google Maps hosts; `norm()` drops anything else.

**Done wishes (Yaptıklarımız).** `memHTML()` draws them as a month-grouped timeline under İstekler; the heading follows the type tab (`WDN`: Gittiklerimiz, Gezdiklerimiz, Okuduklarımız, İzlediklerimiz). Type order everywhere comes from `WT`/`WTAB` (Mekan, Gezi, Kitap, Film). When everyone took part, `allIn()` shows the "Bütün aile" badge instead of avatars.

**Comments on wishes.** Every wish (open or done) shows a chat in its detail sheet; open rows show a comment count (`cmHTML`, `cmList`). Each comment is its own record `wcm/{id} = {w, by, t, at}` (wish id, member, text ≤500), so two phones writing at once don't overwrite each other. Deleting a wish removes its comments. `cmRefresh()` (called from `render()`) redraws an open comment list in place, so incoming comments show up without losing the typed text.

**Books in wishes.** For `kitap` wishes, `wish.book = {t, a, p, y, i}` (title, author, pages, year, cover URL). Typing the title in the wish form searches Google Books (keyless, CORS) and falls back to Open Library (`bkSearch`); picking a result fills it. `okCover()` only accepts https covers from Google Books / Open Library hosts; `norm()` drops anything else. Without a cover, `bkCover()` draws a coloured placeholder.

**Bugün header.** `todayHead()` draws a panel tinted by the hour (`sky-m/d/e/n`) with the greeting (`selam()`: Hayırlı …; Friday, kandil, bayram and Ramazan variants), the Hijri date and moon phase (`hnum()` via Intl `islamic-umalqura`, `moonSVG`), and the next holy day (`holy()`, `nextHoly()`). Hijri dates can be one day off from Diyanet. Under it, `msgStrip()` shows the family's messages one at a time (`msgs/{memberId} = {t, at}`, one per person, kept until its owner removes it; tap = next, pencil = `msgEdit`). An old `ann` counts as its author's message until they write one.

**Şu an burada.** While the app is visible each phone writes `families/{CODE}/on/{memberId} = {at}` every minute (`beat()`) and deletes it when hidden (`beatOff()`, keepalive DELETE). REST has no onDisconnect, so a signal older than 2.5 min counts as gone. `onHereHTML()` shows the others at the top of Bugün and Listeler; `on/` events only repaint that line (`onPaint()`), not the screen. Ayarlar › "Burada olduğumu göster" (`store.prefs.hideOn`) stops this phone from writing. `on` is not in `COLS`, so it never enters `S`/`commit()`.

**Gelişmeler (bell).** The bell next to the avatar on Bugün (`bellBtn`) counts what happened for the current member since they last opened it. `bellItems()` derives the list from existing data (tasks given to me or done by others, events I'm in, wish comments, messages, list items for me, new wishes by others); nothing new is stored remotely. The last-seen time is per device and member in `store.bell[memberId]`. Tapping a row (`bellGo`) closes the sheet and opens that thing. The current `NEWS` also sits in the list as "Uygulamada yenilikler" (`newsOpen`), timed by when this device first saw that version (`store.newsAt`). Tapping the avatar flies it to the member's own card on Aile (`famFly`, skipped with reduced motion).

**Undo.** Un-ticking soon after ticking (task, item, wish rating, "Yapılmadı") calls `unlog()` so the activity entry disappears instead of leaving "aldı" behind; reopening a repeating task removes the next copy it created (`from`). `snap()` captures the flattened state before a change; `toast(msg, undo)` captures the state after it. Undo reverts only the paths that this change touched, so it doesn't clobber edits from other phones.

**Device identity.** Every device signs up anonymously once (`authTok()` with `FB_KEY`, refresh token in `store.auth`), writes itself into `families/{CODE}/uids/{uid}` (`claimUid`, `ensureUid` on every boot) and appends `?auth=<idToken>` to every REST and `EventSource` request. The live rules (`database.rules.json`, applied 2026-10-17) open a family only to the uids listed under it; `meta`/`members` stay readable to any signed-in device so the join screen works, and a device that knows the code can still add its own uid (joining). `database.rules.gecis.json` is the earlier, looser set (families open by code, `avatars` behind uids): paste it in the console to roll back if devices get locked out. Rules are pasted by hand in the Firebase console; editing the file does not deploy them.

**Family avatars.** Family photos are not in the repo; they live under `avatars/{CODE}/{id} = {n,t,d}` (`d` = 256px webp data URL) and are readable only by the family's uids. `loadAv()` fetches the id list (`?shallow=true`) and downloads only missing ones into `store.av` (image) / `store.avi` (person, label); `save()` drops that cache first if localStorage is full. Avatar keys are `ozel/<id>`; `norm()` rewrites old `<prefix>/<id>` values to `ozel/<id>`. New family photos go in through `yukle.html` (same origin, reuses the app's identity from localStorage): it resizes photos to 256px, uploads and deletes them. Never commit family photos to the repo: it is public, and they were purged from its history on 2026-10-17.

**Roles** (`can()`): admin / adult / child. They shape the UI (child view shows only own items, can't assign others) and are not enforced by the rules yet: any registered device can write anything in its family.

**Reminders** are best-effort: `checkReminders()` runs every 30 s while the app is open and shows a notification (via the service worker registration) for the current member's events. Real push would need a server.

**Screen standards** (2026-10-24 review, `/mnt/project-files/bizim-ev/standart-degerlendirme-2026-10-23.md` in the project). Dates only through the helpers: `rel()` in rows (Bugün/Yarın/Dün, a day name for the next six days, else `25 Eki Paz`), `dHead()`/`dHeadTxt()` for day headings, `dFull()` in detail sheets, `ago()` for past times; a task without a time shows nothing, an event shows "Tüm gün". Group headings (`h2.sec`, `.lbl`, `.setsec`, `.rh`, `.tkday`) share one small grey uppercase style; day headings use `.cdh`/`.dhs`. Detail sheets put Düzenle/Sil as header icons via `sheet(title, body, shTools(...))` and keep only the main action at the bottom; tapping an item opens its detail first. Add sheets are titled "Yeni …" with a "… ekle" button, no "(isteğe bağlı)", choices as chips. Wishes are "istek" everywhere. People are listed with `ordMe()` (you first, then the family order). `toastOff()` runs on tab changes.

**Conventions.** All user-facing strings are Turkish; use `toLocaleUpperCase('tr')` / `toLocaleLowerCase('tr')`. Colors are CSS custom properties with light and dark sets (`:root`, `prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-theme]`). Member colors come from `COLORS`. IDs come from `uid()` (time-based base36, always starts with a letter so Firebase never treats a collection as an array).
