# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this is

"Bizim Ev" — a Turkish-language family organizer PWA. The whole app (markup, CSS, JS) lives in `index.html`; there is no build step, package manager or test suite. `sw.js` caches the app shell, `manifest.json` + `icons/` make it installable. Deployed with GitHub Pages from `main` (root).

## Running / developing

- Serve the folder statically (`python3 -m http.server`) and open it. A service worker only registers over http(s), not `file://`.
- Data sync goes to the Firebase Realtime Database in `FB_URL` (top of the "Durum" section). With `FB_URL` empty the app only offers the local "örnek aile" demo.
- For local testing without touching the real database, point `FB_URL` at a small REST+SSE mock (GET/PUT/PATCH on `/families/{code}.json`, and `text/event-stream` GET that sends `put`/`patch` events like Firebase).
- When shipping a change that the service worker must pick up, bump `CACHE` in `sw.js`.
- GitHub Pages' "pages build and deployment" run can hang in its Deploy step (2026-11-05: v121 stuck, failed after 10 min, rerun stayed queued). The live site then keeps the previous version; a new push to `main` starts a fresh deploy. Re-running a "pages build and deployment" run from the API does not help: the re-run stays queued forever and cannot be cancelled.

## Architecture

**Modes.** `store.mode` is `null` (welcome screen), `'demo'` (seeded sample family from `seed()`, local only) or `'family'` (synced). `store` is persisted to localStorage under `bizim-ev.v1` and also caches the last remote tree (`store.R`) and unsent writes (`store.pending`), so the app opens instantly and works offline.

**State and rendering.** `S` is the in-memory view: `{meta, ann, members[], events[], tasks[], lists[], items[], wishes[], activity[], freq[], msgs[]}`. Views are template-string functions (`vToday`, `vCal`, `vLists`/`vWishes`, `vTasks`, `vFamily`) rendered into `#screen` by `render()`. Clicks are delegated through `data-act` → the `A` actions object; form submits through `data-form` → `FORMS`. Bottom sheets (`sheet()`) hold every form and detail view. Mutations change `S` directly and then call `render()`.

**Day agenda.** Bugün and Takvim list days one under another (`flowHTML`; empty days collapse into one dashed row). Tasks sit among events on their due day, sorted by time (`tasksOn`, `cTaskRow`); overdue open tasks move to today and done tasks stay on the day they were done. A sticky week strip (`strip`) sits above the list and `trackStrip()` (on `#screen` scroll and after each render) marks the day at the top; in Takvim it also moves to the next week. The month view lists the month's days under the grid, and tapping a day scrolls to it (`UI.pending` → `scrollToDay`). An event can last several days: `end` is its last day (≤60 days, checked in `norm()`); `evLen`/`evStart`/`evIdx` and `occurs()` (via `occ1`) handle it, repeats included. Flow rows show "2. gün" and when it ends (`evSpan`); reminders and Sıradaki use only the first day. In the month grid, multi-day events and holidays are bars joined across the days of a week (`spanSlots`, `.mb.sp`). Swiping the month grid sideways changes the month, and swiping the flow's week strip changes the week (`MS`/`endMs` → `calMove` / `stripWeek`; both are `touch-action:pan-y`).

**Sync (family mode).** Firebase layout: `families/{CODE}/{meta, ann, members/{id}, events/{id}, tasks/{id}, lists/{id}, items/{id}, wishes/{id}, activity/{id}, freq/{id}, msgs/{memberId}, wcm/{id}}`. `render()` ends with `commit()`, which flattens `S` to `collection/id` paths (`flat()`), diffs each record against the last known version (`baseC`, canonical JSON via `canon()`), queues changed/removed paths in `store.pending`, and `flush()` sends them in one multi-path `PATCH`. Failed sends retry with backoff and on the `online` event. Incoming changes arrive via `EventSource` on the family path (`connect()`); `put`/`patch` events are applied to `store.R`, then `fromRemote()` rebuilds `S` from `store.R` plus `store.pending`. The stream is re-opened (fresh token) when it closes for good (`esLater`, backoff 5–60 s), after more than 10 s in the background, on `online`/`pageshow`, and when nothing (not even Firebase's `keep-alive`) arrived for 2 minutes. Remote data is untrusted: `norm()` validates every record (ids, dates, enums, colors) before it reaches the templates — keep that in place when adding fields, and always `esc()` user text.

**Shared tasks.** A task can have several people in `who` (`assignee` stays `who[0]`, rotation uses it). By default one of them finishing completes it; with `all` each person ticks their own part (`dn`) and it completes when everyone has. Use `ws(t)` / `isFor(t,id)` instead of reading `assignee` directly.

**Görevler header and the top strip.** Bugün / Yaklaşan / Rutinler are tabs (`TKV`, `tkSeg`); Yapılanlar is its own page (`UI.tkb='dn'`, opened by the top-right button or "Bütün yapılanlar", back button "‹ Görevler"). When the big title scrolls out, `#tbar` (absolute in `.phone`, painted by `tbPaint()` from `afterRender` and the `#screen` scroll handler) shows a frosted strip with a small title and the tabs (Microsoft To Do style). Every page has it: `tbIn()` returns per tab the watched element and the strip's content (Bugün: date, weather, bell; Takvim: shown month and view buttons; Alınacaklar/İstekler: coloured title, list/type tabs, cart; Aile: gear; Ev kütüphanesi: back and count). Switching tabs/lists from the strip scrolls to the top. While it shows, `--tbh` on `#screen` moves the sticky week strip under it, and `scrollToDay` adds its height.

**Listeler header.** Alınacaklar and İstekler sit on a camera-style strip (`lTop`, `rtPaint`): the selected one is centred, the other shows faded on both sides, and dragging the strip, tapping the other one, or swiping the list past its first/last sub-tab (`tsTarget` → `rtGo`) switches sides with a spring and a page push. `--buy`/`--wish` colours blend through `--mx` while dragging.

**Adding list items.** Both + buttons (Listeler and Bugün › Alınacak) open the same sheet (`lAddSheet`; `quickItem` just calls it): a name box, a ⋯ button and the + button. ⋯ (`addMore`) opens the details below (`addDet`: list by icon, person like the event/task pickers, `PRI`, note) and hides the suggestions; the hint line (`addHint`) sums up what was picked. `addApply()` puts the details on the next item(s) added and resets them; the chosen list stays. Suggestions under the name box (`suggHTML`) are rows, not chips: up to five, each with how long ago it was bought and its average interval (`sgInfo`: newest done item's `doneAt`, else `freq.last`; `freq.every`). Items past their average get a "Vakti geldi" tag and come first.

**Shared products (Hepsiburada).** The same manifest `share_target` also takes products: `parseHb()` accepts a shared text/URL only when the host passes `okHbUrl()` (hepsiburada.com, hb.biz, https), takes the name from the text (else the URL slug before `-p-`), and the boot code opens `hbForm()` (name, list, the link) so the name can be fixed before it is added; `parseHb` strips "- Hepsiburada" suffixes and generic words like "Paylaş" (`clean`/`bad`), an empty name has to be typed, and the form has a tap-to-open "Paylaşımla gelen metin" line with the raw title/text for debugging. `hbAdd` then adds it to the chosen list with `item.link` (`norm()` drops links that fail `okHbUrl`). The item detail shows "Hepsiburada'da aç". Android only: iOS PWAs are not share targets. Price/picture cannot be read from the page.

**Bought items.** Under each list, "Alınanlar" (collapsed, `UI.doneOpen`) groups bought items by when they were bought (`donePer`: Bu hafta, Geçen hafta, Bu ay, then month names; no `doneAt` → Daha eski). The newest group is open, the others are closed and show the first names (`dpPrev`); open/closed per group is `UI.dpo`, reset on list change. Rows (`dnRow`) say who bought it (`doneBy`, set by `toggleItem`, string-checked in `norm()`) and the day (`dDay`); the item detail shows the same.

**Item prices.** An item can carry `item.cost` (estimated price, whole TL, `parseTL()` reads "1.250 TL"; `norm()` keeps 0 < cost < 1e8, else drops it). It is entered in the add sheet's ⋯ details ("Tahmini fiyat") or the item form, shown on rows (`.cst`) and in the detail. Under the list, `.cstot` sums the pending items (`cSum`) and says how many have no price; it only appears when at least one pending item has a price.

**Trip plans.** `yer` (Gezi) wishes may carry `wish.trip = {g, b, k:{u,k,y,g}, r[], v, m}` (days, budget, budget split ulaşım/konaklama/yemek/gezi from `TK`, route stops, transport from `TV`, season from `TM`); `norm()` clamps the numbers, keeps ≤30 stops of ≤80 chars and drops the field on other types. In the form `tripForm()` stays a single "Gezi planı ekle" row until tapped; the split budget is behind "Kalemlere ayır". The detail (`tripHTML`) shows the total and a bar, and the split legend opens on tap (`tpBud`). Rows show `tripSum()` ("3 gün · 18.000 ₺"); "Rotayı haritada aç" is a plain Google Maps directions URL (`routeUrl`). General rule from the user: avoid crowding, so extra details open on tap.

**Places in wishes.** For `mekan`/`yer` wishes, `wish.place = {n, a, u}` (name, address, Google Maps link). It arrives through the manifest `share_target` (Google Maps → Paylaş → Bizim Ev opens `./?text=…`, parsed by `parsePlace()` at boot) or a pasted link in the wish form. No Maps API key: detail buttons use plain Google Maps URLs (`mapsUrl`, `dirUrl`). `okMapsUrl()` only accepts https Google Maps hosts; `norm()` drops anything else.

**Done wishes (Yaptıklarımız).** `memHTML()` draws them as a month-grouped timeline under İstekler; the heading follows the type tab (`WDN`: Gittiklerimiz, Gezdiklerimiz, Okuduklarımız, İzlediklerimiz). Type order everywhere comes from `WT`/`WTAB` (Mekan, Gezi, Kitap, Film). When everyone took part, `allIn()` shows the "Bütün aile" badge instead of avatars.

**Plan answers.** "Plana dök" asks the adults it adds (not the planner, not children): the event gets `ask` (member ids), `askAt` and `wish`. Each answer is its own record `rsv/{id} = {e, m, v, at, d, t}` (`v`: y katılıyor, n katılamıyor, a başka gün önerdi with `d`/`t`), so two phones answering at once don't overwrite each other; `rsvOf(e)` takes each person's latest answer newer than `askAt`. The plan is in the calendar at once (Google Takvim style). Unanswered plans show as a card at the top of Bugün (`rsvCards`, `rsvAsk`) and in the bell; rows show `rsvTag()`. "Katılamam" also removes the person from `members`. The planner sees who said what in the event detail (`rsvPeople`) and can move the plan to a suggested day (`rsvTake`), which sets a new `askAt` so the others are asked again; editing the date/time does the same. Deleting the event removes its `rsv` records.

**Comments on wishes.** Every wish (open or done) shows a chat in its detail sheet; open rows show a comment count (`cmHTML`, `cmList`). Each comment is its own record `wcm/{id} = {w, by, t, at}` (wish id, member, text ≤500), so two phones writing at once don't overwrite each other. Deleting a wish removes its comments. Books in the home library use the same records (`w` = book id). `cmRefresh()` (called from `render()`) redraws an open comment list in place, so incoming comments show up without losing the typed text.

**Event comments and memory photos.** Every event detail (`openEvent` → `evMem`) has the wish chat: `wcm` records with `w` = `evKey(e, day)` (the event id, or `id_YYYYMMDD` for repeating events so each occurrence has its own talk; `evOfK` reverses it). Once the event is over (`evDone`) photos can be added: cover + small ones (`evPhotos`), full screen viewer with delete (`phShow`), and the Takvim/Bugün row shows the first photo's thumbnail or a comment count (`evTh` in `crow`). Photos are NOT in `COLS`: they live at `photos/{CODE}/{id} = {m, t, d}` (m = JSON `{e, by, at}`, t = 320 px, d = 1280 px JPEG data URLs; ids random like avatars, `newAvId`, so they do not leak the time) so the live stream never carries them; `phLoad()` reads the id list shallow (on boot, opening an event, at most every 5 min from `afterRender`), fetches only `m`/`t` of new ids, `phFull()` fetches `d` on view. Cache: IndexedDB `bizim-ev-foto` (+ `PH` in memory), never localStorage. In a locked family each field is sealed (`phSeal`/`phOpen`, AAD `ph/<id>/<field>`, image as mime + 0 + bytes like `avSeal`), and nothing is added unless the key is verified. Max 30 per event; deleting an event deletes its comments and photos (undo brings back the event and comments only). The live rules are `database.rules.kilit.json` (the family is locked); its `photos` block also requires `e1.` values in a locked family.

**Memory reminder card.** `hmCard()` at the top of Bugün asks "… nasıldı?" for the newest event I took part in that ended (`evDone`, so ~1 h after a timed event) within the last 3 days and where I have no photo or comment yet (`hmNext`/`hmDue`). Buttons: Fotoğraf ekle (`phPick`) and Yorum yaz (`hmCm` opens the event and focuses the chat). The × (`hmX`) hides it on this phone only (`store.hmx`, pruned after 8 days); nothing goes to Firebase.

**Hatıralarımız (album).** A card on Aile under Ev kütüphanesi (`albCard`) opens `UI.tab='album'` (`vAlb`, back to Aile, `tbIn` strip). `albEvs()` collects events that have photos in `PH` (newest first); the page shows horizontal month collage cards (`.amc`; tapping filters to that month, `UI.albM` via `albM`) and event rows that open the event (`openEvent` with the day). It only reads what `phLoad()` has already cached; opening it calls `phLoad(15e3)`.

**Home library (Ev kütüphanesi).** A card on Aile (`libCard`, under the selected member) opens its own page, `UI.tab='lib'` (`vLib`, back button to Aile; + scans). Records `books/{id} = {t, a, p, y, k, i, c, isbn, rd[], by, at}`; `norm()` checks them (`LKAT` category, else `dg` Diğer; `okCover`; `c` spine colour hex; `okIsbn` = EAN-13 978/979 with checksum). Adding: "Barkod tara" (`libScan` → `scStart`: camera, `BarcodeDetector` where it reads EAN-13, else `vendor/zxing-0.21.3.min.js` loaded on first scan; the loop stops when the video leaves the page or the app is hidden) looks the ISBN up with `bkIsbn` (all sources at once, first hit by priority: Google `isbn:` 13 and 10 (`isbn10`), a plain number search kept only if an identifier matches, Open Library bibkeys and `search.json`; a missing cover is borrowed from another hit); if nothing has it, `libFind` opens with the ISBN and a Kitapyurdu link. The scanner asks for 1920×1080, `scTune()` sets continuous focus, zoom ≤2 and a torch button (`#sctorch`, Android only; iPhone Safari offers none), ZXing alternates crops and binarizers per frame, and non-ISBN barcodes get a hint; "Adla ara" (`libFind`) uses `bkSearch`, and if nothing fits adds the typed name. `katGuess()` guesses the category from the API subjects (novel + Turkish-looking author → Türk edebiyatı); it can be changed in the detail. `libHas()` stops duplicates; `libLog()` folds several adds into one activity line. The spine colour is the cover's main colour read on the adding phone (`coverTint`, CORS copy with `bzt=1` so the SW's opaque cover cache doesn't block it), else a colour from the title (`bcol`). The page: ring card with my read share, tapping Okuduklarım/Okumadıklarım filters (`UI.lf`); sliders button → Sırala sheet (`DZ`, kept in `store.prefs.lsrt`); shelves per category with spines (`spine`, width from pages, green dot = I read it); the button next to the sliders (`lbTog`, `store.prefs.lv`) switches to a list (`lbList`/`lbRow`: small cover, full title, author, green dot), still grouped by category. Holding a spine ~0.4 s (`LP`, `lpShow`; capture-phase pointer listeners, the following click is eaten) blurs the page and grows the book from the spine to the centre with its cover (`lpCover`: the real cover, else a drawn `.mkc` cover in the spine colour), title, author and Ayrıntılar; tapping outside or Escape shrinks it back (`lpHide`); reduced motion skips the movement. Categories: `LKAT` = 18 types like Kitapyurdu's main headings (`[name, colour, icon]`; `dt` is the old "Düşünce ve tarih", kept for books still in it but not offered), `katGuess()` maps API subjects to them. `reKat()` (from `vLib`, family mode, once per phone: `store.libK`) re-guesses books still in the old five keys (`KOLD`) from Google's volume record (id from the cover URL, else ISBN) or Open Library's subjects, plus title words (`katT`: şiir, hikâye, tarih, sözlük, yemek, anı); a plain novel guess only moves books out of Diğer / Düşünce ve tarih. Detail (`libSheet`): category as one row that opens the list on tap (`lkPick`, `F.lkOpen`, `libKatTog`/`libKat`; adder or adults), Kim okudu (everyone marks only themselves, others are shown locked), comments = the wish chat (`wcm` with `w` = book id; bell and "sen yokken" list them). Deleting a book removes its comments. New titles and authors go through `tcase(t, lang)` (every word and hyphen part capitalised, Roman numerals kept; an ALL CAPS or all lower title is re-cased, a mixed one keeps its letters; locale = the source's language (`gbMap` `lang`), else `tr` if it has Turkish letters, else `en` so English "in/it" don't become "İn/İt"; a Turkish ALL CAPS title without Turkish letters guesses I/ı by vowel harmony, `trI`). `bkIsbn` takes the Turkish-lettered spelling of the same title/author from another source when the first hit is ASCII-folded. `store.libTc` = 3 repairs once per device the word-initial İ an older conversion put into titles without Turkish letters (`tcFix`); "Düzenle" in the detail (`libEdit`) keeps the title as typed. Cover sizes: stored `i` is the small source image (Google `zoom=1` ≈128 px, Open Library `-M`). `cvL(b, w)` lists candidates small → best: `i`, Open Library by ISBN (`/b/isbn/<isbn>-L.jpg?default=false`, 404 when missing), OL `-L` for OL ids, and Google's publisher cover `/books/publisher/content/images/frontcover/<id>?fife=w<w>-h<h>` (id read from `i`); `cvImg` stacks them so the small one shows at once and the best one that loads sits on top (failed or <40 px ones remove themselves). `CVW`: md 320, lg 600; xs/sm use only `i`, lazy. Long press starts `cvPre(b, 600)` on pointerdown; `cvWarmUp()` fetches all small covers once when the library opens. The SW cover cache (`bizim-ev-kapak2`, 600 entries) fetches covers with CORS so entries aren't opaque (opaque ones count ~7 MB each against storage quota). Turkish letters: titles/authors that came ASCII-folded ("yanki ustasi", "Ihsan Oktay Anar") go through `trFix(b, lang, net)` after `libNew` (first a `bkSearch` hit with the same folded spelling and Turkish letters, then `deasc()`: a port of Deniz Yuret's turkish-mode deasciifier using the decision lists in `vendor/tr-harf.json`, loaded on demand by `dscLoad`; words in `DSK` (Ali, King, Tolkien, Dune…) are kept). It only runs for Turkish books (`trBook`: ISBN 978-975/605/9944, source lang tr, shelf `tr`) or titles without English words (`ENW`); all-lower, all-caps or sentence-case names are then re-cased with `tcase`. `trRepair()` does the same offline once per device for existing books (`store.libTc` = 4). Category names can be renamed by adults by tapping the shelf heading (`katEdit`): `bkat/{key} = {n, at}`, read through `katN(k)`; empty = default name.

**Reading habit (okuma alışkanlığı).** The library page starts with a reading card (`okuCard`): one line by default (ring with today's minutes against a daily goal, the streak `okuStreak` with a flame, Okudum; "Yeni basamak hazır" when a level-up waits); tapping the text or the arrow (`okuTog`, kept per phone in `store.prefs.okc`) opens this week's days (`okuWeek`) and four levels `OKUL` Filiz/Fidan/Ağaç/Çınar (adults 10/15/20/30 dk, children 5/10/15/20). "Okudum" (`okuLog` → `okuLogBody`) picks a book (current one first, then unread), minutes (`icGrid`) and "Bu kitabı bitirdim" (adds me to `book.rd`, activity line); undo via `toast(…, snap())`. Records: `okl/{id} = {m, d, dk, g, b, at}` (one per log so two phones don't overwrite; `g` is that day's goal so a level change doesn't break old days; each phone prunes its own logs older than 400 days) and `okh/{memberId} = {lv, b, at}` (`okuSetH`). Level-up is offered when today's goal was reached on 5 of the last 7 days (`okuRdy`, `okuUp`); adults set their own and the children's level in the goal sheet (`okuSet`, `okuCan`), children see it locked. Aile cards show a streak badge (`okuBadge`) and the library card shows today's minutes. Both collections are in `COLS`, `norm()` and the lock's `LKG`, and have `e1.` rules in `database.rules.kilit.json`.

**Medicine cabinet (İlaç dolabı).** A card on Aile under Hatıralarımız (`ilCard`) opens `UI.tab='ilac'` (`vIlac`, back to Aile, `tbIn` strip, + and the nav + open `ilForm`). Records `ilac/{id} = {n, c, s, k, a, no, g, by, at}` (name, type `IKAT`, expiry `YYYY-MM` or empty (`okSkt`), for whom (member id, empty = everyone), boxes 1–99, note, GTIN-14 (`okGtin`)); in `COLS`, `norm()`, the lock's `LKG` and an `e1.` rule in `database.rules.kilit.json`. The page lists Tarihi geçmiş / Yakında bitiyor (end of month within 62 days, `ilSt`) / Dolapta, sorted by date (Apple Health style). Adding: the sheet opens the camera (`scStart('il')`: `scMode` makes `scReader` look for `data_matrix` + `ean_13`; ZXing gets DATA_MATRIX) and `ilGot` → `ilParse` reads the Turkish İTS karekod (GS1: 01 GTIN, 21 serial, 17 expiry YYMMDD, 10 lot, GS = char 29) into the form and closes the camera. The name and type come from `vendor/ilac-adlari.tsv` (`ilDb`, fetched once when the add sheet opens; ~23k rows `barcode13 TAB name TAB type-from-ATC`), built from TİTCK's "Ruhsatlı Beşeri Tıbbi Ürünler Listesi" xlsx (titck.gov.tr/dinamikmodul/85; the cloud can't reach it, the user downloads and uploads it) with `/mnt/project-files/bizim-ev/ilac-listesi/ilac-listesi.py` (Turkish casing: a row with İ uses I = ı, else I = i; the first word's I is always i). Family records with the same GTIN and the phone's own `store.ilg` (GTIN → name, ≤300, never sent) come first (`ilName`); a medicine not in the list is typed once and remembered. Same GTIN + expiry + person adds boxes instead of a new record. Type is guessed from the name (`ilGuess`) unless picked in the tap-to-open row (`ilKat`). Detail (`ilSheet`): Düzenle/Sil (adder or adults), "Bitti / Attım, yenisini alınacaklara ekle" (`ilDone`: removes it and adds the name to an Eczane/İlaç list or the first list, with undo). Rows and the detail title show only the brand (`ilShort` via `ilSplit`/`ILF`: words before the first strength/form word); mg, ml, form and count show under the title in the detail (`ilRest`). Kimin için (`ilForChips`, box `il-for`, actions `ilFor`/`ilPick`) offers Bütün aile and, when the family has children, Yetişkinler / Çocuklar (`k` = `yetiskin` / `cocuk`) before the faces; a scanned name with pediatri/çocuk/bebek preselects Çocuklar. The page has a search box (`#il-q`, `UI.iq`, `ilList`/`ilPaint` redraw only the list; matches name, note, type, person). The expiry field is a `.dtp` box (hidden `type=month` input; `dtSync` handles month) because iPhone's own month input grew past the screen. `ilChip()` puts a red "N ilacın tarihi geçmiş / yaklaşıyor" pill in the greeting panel next to the acil pill.

**Mutfak (kitchen).** Side-menu row (`drwItems`, first) opens `UI.tab='mutfak'` (`vMut`, back to the tab it came from `UI.muFrom`/`MUFR`, `tbIn` strip with the tabs). Tabs (`UI.mt`, `muSeg`): Bu hafta (`muWeek`: today + 6 days, each day's dishes and "Yemek ekle / Bir yemek daha") and Tarifler (`muList`, search `#mu-q`/`UI.muq`, grouped by `MUK`). Records: `tarif/{id} = {t, k, dk, s, m[], y[], l, by, at}` (name, type `MUK`, minutes, servings, ingredient lines ≤60×120, steps ≤40×500, https source link `muOk`) and `mplan/{id} = {d, r, n, w, by, at}` (day, recipe id or `n` = dish name without a recipe, cook); one record per dish so a day can hold several and two phones don't overwrite each other. Both in `COLS`, `norm()`, the lock's `LKG` ("Mutfak") and `e1.` rules in `database.rules.kilit.json`. Each phone deletes its own plans older than 30 days (`muPrune`). Recipe detail (`muOpen`/`muBody`): source row, Malzemeler/Yapılışı tabs, portion stepper (`muPor`); ingredient lines are parsed by `muIng` (leading quantity incl. 1,5 · 1/2 · 2-3 · yarım, then a unit from `MUU`) and scaled with `muQty`/`muFmt` (gram/ml to tens, pieces to halves). "Malzemeleri alınacaklara ekle" (`muBuy`) opens a checklist (list picked in one tap-to-open row, items already pending in that list start unticked), `muBuyGo` adds each as an item named after the ingredient with the amount and recipe in `note`, with undo. Adding a dish (`muAdd`): search/pick several recipes or type a name (`muFree`, Enter picks the single match), cook via `faceGrid`; a dish row opens `muPl` (cook, Tarifi aç, Plandan çıkar). Deleting a recipe keeps its plans as named dishes. Bugün shows "Bu akşam" (`muToday`, after `hmCard`) once the family has any recipe or plan. Recipe form `muForm` (Kendim yazayım / Linkten kaydet, type row `muKP`); links are stored only as links (no copying from the site). **Sheet height (çekmece boyu).** `sheet()` calls `shHold()`: a ResizeObserver on `.sb` keeps the tallest height seen in `--shh` (CSS `min-height`, capped like `max-height`), so a sheet never shrinks while open (tab switches, filters, collapsing rows) and growth animates (min/max-height, skipped with reduced motion). `.grip` and `.sh` don't flex-shrink (a shrunk grip painted as a block in Chrome).

**Undo.** Un-ticking soon after ticking (task, item, wish rating, "Yapılmadı") calls `unlog()` so the activity entry disappears instead of leaving "aldı" behind; reopening a repeating task removes the next copy it created (`from`). `snap()` captures the flattened state before a change; `toast(msg, undo)` captures the state after it. **Device identity.** Every device signs up anonymously once (`authTok()` with `FB_KEY`, refresh token in `store.auth`), writes itself into `families/{CODE}/uids/{uid}` (`claimUid`, `ensureUid` on every boot) and appends `?auth=<idToken>` to every REST and `EventSource` request. The live rules (`database.rules.json`, applied 2026-10-17) open a family only to the uids listed under it; `meta`/`members` stay readable to any signed-in device so the join screen works, and a device that knows the code can still add its own uid (joining). `database.rules.gecis.json` is the earlier, looser set (families open by code, `avatars` behind uids): paste it in the console to roll back if devices get locked out. Rules are pasted by hand in the Firebase console; editing the file does not deploy them.

**End-to-end lock (uçtan uca kilit).** A family can be locked so Firebase only holds ciphertext. Every record (each `COLS` record, `meta`, `ann`) becomes the string `"e1." + b64url(iv12‖AES-256-GCM ciphertext)`, with AAD `code + '/' + path`. The key is 32 random bytes kept on each phone in `store.fk` (never sent); `families/{CODE}/k = {v, st, by, at, chk, w}` holds `st` (`on`, `migrating`, `undo`), `chk` (`{ok:1}` encrypted, to verify a key) and `w` (the key wrapped with the 12-character recovery code via PBKDF2-SHA256 600k). Decrypted records are cached in `store.D[path] = {i: iv, v}`; `flatR()` reads through `rv()`, so a record this phone cannot open never enters `S`/`baseC` and `commit()` never deletes it. `remote()` runs `keyState()` → `decAll()` → `sweep()` (re-encrypts plaintext found in a locked family). `flush()` checks `/k` once per stream (`KS.fresh`), holds every write while a lock exists and the key isn't verified or `st !== 'on'`, and encrypts the body (`encBody`) when locked. Phones without a working key get `vKeyless()` (QR scan via `BarcodeDetector`/ZXing `QRCodeReader`, pasted link, recovery code; one `F.kq` flow shared with joining a locked family, `joinOpen`/`joinLocked`). Key hand-over link: `?aile=CODE#k=KEY` (`keyUrl`, QR drawn by `qrSvg`); boot reads `#k=` into `KS.lk` and wipes it from the address bar. Admin lock flow (`lockStart` → `lockGo` version gate on `ver/{uid}` = `APPV` → `lockPrep` backup to IndexedDB `bizim-ev-yedek` → `rcSheet`/`rcCheck` → `lockRun`: `lkReid` renames avatar ids to random ones, PUT `k` st `migrating`, `lkRecords('enc')` in verified passes, `lkAvatars('enc')` (header + 0 + image bytes, AAD `av/<id>`), `lkPhotos('enc')` (event photos one by one: seal, write, read back and compare; time-based ids move to random ones), st `on`). `lkUndoRun` reverses it. Event photos are only loaded once the key is verified (`phLoad` waits; `keyState` resets `phAt`). New families are created locked when lock is available. `LOCK_ON` is false until lock day; `?kilit=1` or tapping Ayarlar › Eşitleme five times (`syncTap`) turns it on for one phone (`store.prefs.lkOn`). `database.rules.kilit.json` (live since 2026-10-31) makes the rules reject plaintext records in a locked family. `yukle.html` seals/opens avatars the same way and uses random ids.

**Family avatars.** Family photos are not in the repo; they live under `avatars/{CODE}/{id} = {n,t,d}` (`d` = 256px webp data URL) and are readable only by the family's uids. `loadAv()` fetches the id list (`?shallow=true`) and downloads only missing ones into `store.av` (image) / `store.avi` (person, label); `save()` drops that cache first if localStorage is full. Avatar keys are `ozel/<id>`; `norm()` rewrites old `<prefix>/<id>` values to `ozel/<id>`. New family photos are added in the app: the avatar sheet (`avPick`) starts with the member's big avatar and a camera badge (`afPick` → hidden `#afin` file input, camera or gallery), `afStart` shows a circular crop (drag, pinch, wheel, slider; `afAuto` centres on a face where `FaceDetector` exists, else the upper part of a portrait), `afOut` draws 256px webp (JPEG on iPhone, white background), `afStore` PUTs `avatars/{CODE}/{newAvId()}` (sealed with `avSeal` when the family is locked and the key checks out) and `setAv` makes it that member's avatar. `yukle.html` (same origin, reuses the app's identity from localStorage) still uploads in bulk and is the only place to delete photos. Never commit family photos to the repo: it is public, and they were purged from its history on 2026-10-17.

**Roles** (`can()`): admin / adult / child. They shape the UI (child view shows only own items, can't assign others) and are not enforced by the rules yet: any registered device can write anything in its family.

**iPhone status bar colour.** On iOS only (`IOS`), `stbar()` rewrites the single `theme-color` meta to the colour actually under the status bar: `--bg` of the in-app theme, blended with an open sheet's `.scrim` / `.popov.on` / `.apo.on` background, black for the photo viewer `.phv`. Sheets stop below the inset.
